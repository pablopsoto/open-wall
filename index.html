    About

    Every work here is in the public domain. Open access means out of copyright, so roughly before 1931; this room hangs the modern end of that window: paintings from 1860, prints and drawings from 1880. They come from the open-access collections of seven museums: the Cleveland Museum of Art, the National Gallery of Art, SMK, the National Gallery of Denmark, the Belvedere in Vienna (photographs CC BY-SA 4.0), the Rijksmuseum, the Finnish National Gallery and Nationalmuseum in Stockholm.
